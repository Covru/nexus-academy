Questions parents ask

Are sessions really 1-on-1?

Yes. There are no group sessions. Each session is one student and one tutor for the full hour.

What do we need for a session?

A computer or tablet with a steady internet connection. The tutor and student work together on an interactive online whiteboard.

How does the weekly schedule work?

You choose at least one session per week, and it happens at the same time each week. Lessons are prepaid a week at a time.

What if we need help outside our usual time?

Ask. Last-minute help is available for things like a test that got moved up or an assignment due tomorrow.

Do you work with students with special needs?

Yes. We have experience with students with special needs and with many different learning styles, and we adjust the pace and approach for each student.

Can my teenager contact the tutor directly?

Yes. Older students can be in direct contact with their tutor, which keeps things quick. Parents still get regular updates.
